# A. Markdown Prompt Playbook

## The Davao City Health Office Prompt System

#### 1. System Prompt Template (V3 - Final Optimized)

"Act as a Chief Clinical Psychologist and Public Health Consultant specializing in community mental health delivery in the Davao Region. Your objective is to draft a 300-word operational mobilization framework to address severe shortages of registered mental health professionals in Davao City.

Context: Rapidly rising mental health consultation requests across urban districts are overwhelming the few available city psychologists, leading to critical service bottlenecks for low-income residents.

Constraints: Use an urgent, professional, and solutions-oriented tone. Do NOT suggest long-term university degree paths or foreign recruitment strategies. Focus entirely on immediate local capacity building, including task-shifting protocols for Barangay Health Workers (BHWs), integrating basic psychological first aid into existing city programs, and partnering with local university psychology departments for supervised graduate internships. Avoid academic clinical jargon; write in direct, accessible English.

Format: Output in clear Markdown with exactly three immediate operational strategies under the heading '### Strategic Mental Health Workforce Mobilization'."

#### 2. Prompt Battle Ledger

| Version | Prompt Modifier Added | Output Quality Reflection |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **V1** | "Write a plan to fix the shortage of public mental health workers in Davao." | Too vague. The AI suggested multi-year government salary scaling and building new psychiatric hospitals, which fails to solve the immediate human resource bottleneck. |
| **V2** | "Act as a clinical advisor in Davao City. Write a deployment plan for psychological professionals to urban centers." | Better context, but the response assumed a large pool of available licensed professionals already existed. It completely missed the reality that the actual talent pool is highly limited. |
| **V3** | Added strict focus on community task-shifting, localized university internship pipelines, and immediate BHW capacity building while excluding long-term structural overhauls. | **Target hit.** Delivers a highly pragmatic, low-cost approach tailored specifically to maximizing limited personnel assets within Davao City. |

#### 3. Visual Branding Asset

* **Engine Used:** Canva Magic Media
* **Visual Prompt:** "A flat minimalist vector logo featuring a central brain icon surrounded by human figures arranged in a circular formation. One side of the circle is expanding outward with new figures and a plus sign, symbolizing the active recruitment of public mental health workers. Clean geometric design, modern government health program style. Use dark blue, green, and white. White background. No text, gradients, shadows, or 3D effects."
